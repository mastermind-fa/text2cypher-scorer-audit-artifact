## B. The policy

**P1 — Joint continuation.** Both models are continued because each independently passed its original frozen floor (Qwen 11/40, Mistral 8/40, both ≥ 6/40). Both are run once on `packet_mtq_remaining260.jsonl` with unchanged frozen settings. Any paired scorer-impact analysis is reported only if **both** models complete all 260 outputs; if either run is incomplete, the existing outputs are reported as incomplete, no paired claim is made, and content is not rerun. Infrastructure interruptions may be continued only via the documented subset-packet merge (completed rows never regenerated). *Post-Gate40 decision, applies prospectively to the 260.*

**P2 — Primary metric and sensitivity.** The frozen project-defined single-instance execution-result equivalence is primary for all accuracy statements, **including empty and all-NULL matches**. A separate **sensitivity analysis** excludes *units* whose **reference** result is empty (zero rows) or all-NULL (≥ 1 row, every cell NULL). The rule is a property of the unit, applied identically to every scorer regardless of that scorer's verdict. Zero-aggregate (all-zero) results are *not* excluded; their count is reported. Candidate execution errors are never excluded from the primary denominator. Both views are reported for every scorer, side by side. Exclusion never replaces the primary result.

**P3 — Uncertainty and ranking claims.** Frozen: paired **question-level** and **near-cluster-level** bootstrap 95% intervals (10,000, seed 55), exact McNemar secondary, discordant counts. A **strong model-ranking claim** (Qwen vs Mistral) is made only if, on the pooled 300 outputs, (a) primary and sensitivity views agree in direction; (b) the question- and cluster-level 95% intervals for Qwen − Mistral exclude 0 in both views; and (c) the direction is the same in `er` and in the other graphs pooled. **Graph-domain caution:** condition (c) is assessed only when each side (`er`; non-`er` pooled) has **≥ 30 paired comparable questions** (a number introduced after Gate40, not from the frozen protocol); otherwise (c) is reported "not assessable", with counts, and the ranking is described at most as *tentative / er-dominated*, never strong. Failing these conditions does **not** invalidate descriptive scorer-impact findings (counts, disagreements, mechanisms), which are reported regardless, labelled descriptive. Gate40's ordering is not evidence for the ranking.

**P4 — Scope.** The extension is restricted to the frozen Mind the Query 300-question packet (Gate40 + remaining 260). No second benchmark is launched. No cross-benchmark generalisation claim is made. This is disclosed as a deviation from the two-benchmark plan, whose second benchmark never passed its licence/execution gate.

**P5 — Graph coverage (bloom).** Before scoring, attempt to provision `bloom` (dump `bloom-50.dump`, SHA-256 `926859ea…19e5`, hash-verified) as a **fourth separate** Community instance under the S55F amendment and identity rule S55G-ID-v1. If restoration or any security/identity check fails, the affected questions (2 `bloom` IDs) are marked **"not evaluated — graph unavailable"**, keep their original IDs, and are reported as incomplete graph coverage. They are not model failures, not silently excluded, not substituted. For every model and every scorer report: coverage counts (evaluable / not evaluated / candidate execution errors), observed accuracy over evaluable units k/n_eval, and bounds against the full intended denominator of 260: **lower = k/260, upper = (k + u)/260**, u = not-evaluated units. No claim of a complete-benchmark result is made unless all required graphs are evaluable.

**P6 — Adjudication sample.** The existing 11-case blinded packet (SHA `358a34e9…`) is preserved untouched and remains Gate40-only. For the 260 outputs, the **case-selection algorithm and seed are frozen before inference** in `code/select_p6_sample_55k.py` (SHA recorded in the inference manifest), tested on synthetic data only; actual case IDs are selected only after scorer verdicts exist. Definitions: *Verdict columns* = the frozen project metric + the 9 replayed implementations. *Comparable unit* = (model, question) whose project-metric status is `supported_scored`. *Eligible* = comparable and all 10 columns `supported_scored` with verdict 0/1. *Unsupported/exception/missing* scorer outcomes make a comparable unit **ineligible**: it is listed in an `ineligible` register with reasons, reported, and not sampled. *Agreement* = eligible and all 10 verdicts equal; *disagreement* = eligible and not all equal. *Duplicates*: units with the same question ID and the same whitespace/case-normalised predicted query collapse into one case with all member units recorded. *Sample* = **all** disagreement cases + the first **20** distinct agreement cases ordered by SHA-256("S55K-P6|seed55|" + model + "|" + question_id) ascending, skipping already-selected cases; if fewer than 20 exist, take all and record the shortfall. Blinding, A/B randomisation and the annotation protocol follow the 55I handoff. **Two independent annotations with documented conflict resolution are required before any statement about semantic correctness**; until then such statements are labelled *unadjudicated*. No labels are fabricated. (Empty/all-NULL agreement units are not separately stratified; any added stratum would be a new, separately approved amendment.)

## C. What this policy does not do
It does not authorise inference, provisioning, scoring, any manuscript integration, or any change to frozen models, decoding, prompts, packets, metric or the 6/40 floor.
